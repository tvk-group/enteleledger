# EnteleLEDGER brand asset usage

The approved EnteleLEDGER mark combines layered records, an immutable ledger spine, an **L** foundation and a verification checkpoint. The public wordmark is always written `EnteleLEDGER`.

## Files

- `/assets/brand/logo-dark.svg` — full logo for light surfaces.
- `/assets/brand/logo-light.svg` — full logo for dark surfaces.
- `/assets/brand/mark-dark.svg` — standalone mark for light surfaces.
- `/assets/brand/mark-light.svg` — standalone mark for dark surfaces.
- `/assets/brand/icon.svg` — favicon and PWA source.
- `/assets/brand/og-image.svg` — social preview source.

## Rules

1. Preserve clear space equal to the verification seal diameter around the full logo.
2. Do not recolour, skew, outline, re-typeset or rearrange the approved artwork.
3. Use full wordmark artwork when the standalone mark could be ambiguous.
4. Store SVG files locally. Do not hot-link identity assets.
5. Keep the accessible name `EnteleLEDGER` on linked logos and hide decorative duplicates from assistive technology.
6. Public development material must not imply live custody, issuance, customer accounts or value movement.
